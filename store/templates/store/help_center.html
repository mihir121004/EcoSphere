Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order? +
To place an order, browse our products, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You'll need to create an account or log in if you haven't already.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), UPI payments, and credit/debit card payments for your convenience.
How can I track my order? +
Once your order is placed, you can track it using the order tracking feature on your profile page or by using the order ID provided in your confirmation email.
What is your return policy? +
We offer returns within 30 days of delivery for unused items in their original packaging. Please refer to our Returns & Refunds page for detailed information.
How do I change my password? +
You can change your password from your profile page. Go to your profile, click on the "Change Password" tab, and follow the instructions.

Still need help?

If you couldn't find the answer to your question, feel free to contact us.
