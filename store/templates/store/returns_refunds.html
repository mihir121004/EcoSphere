Returns & Refunds

Our policy on returns, exchanges, and refunds

Return Policy

Return Window: You can return items within 30 days of delivery.
Condition: Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and with all tags attached.

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you're not happy with your order, you can return it for a full refund or exchange.

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Contact our customer support team with your order number and reason for return.
  2. Receive a return authorization and shipping label.
  3. Pack the item securely in its original packaging.
  4. Ship the item back to us using the provided label.
  5. Once received, we'll process your refund or exchange within 5-7 business days.

Refund Process

Refunds are processed to the original payment method. Please allow 5-7 business days for the refund to appear in your account. Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

Exchanges

If you'd like to exchange an item for a different size, color, or product, we'll be happy to assist. Exchange shipping costs may apply depending on the circumstances.

Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:
  • Personal care items
  • Custom or personalized products
  • Items damaged due to misuse
  • Items without original packaging or tags

Contact Us

If you have any questions about returns or need to initiate a return, please contact our customer support team. We're here to help!
